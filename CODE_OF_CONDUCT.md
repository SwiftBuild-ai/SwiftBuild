# Community code of conduct

Treat contributors respectfully regardless of background, identity, experience, or viewpoint. Give specific, constructive feedback and assume good intent. Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, unwanted sexual attention, doxxing, and publication of private information are unacceptable.

Maintainers may remove harmful content, restrict participation, or ban repeated offenders. Enforcement should be proportionate and consider context. Maintainers must establish a private conduct-reporting channel before opening community participation; no contact has been supplied yet. Never post private incident details in public issues.

This policy applies to project spaces and official representation of SwiftBuild.
